    Sources, APIs & Local Proxy Setup

    How this unified reader connects to open catalogs, supports offline files, and streams MangaDex & Internet Archive binaries.

    7 Live Integrated APIs

    Active
    • Gutendex (gutendex.com) — Project Gutenberg catalog of 70,000+ public domain books with full in-app EPUB/TXT reading.
    • Open Library (openlibrary.org) — Trending daily books, search, covers, and Internet Archive book embeds.
    • Google Books (googleapis.com/books) — Broad catalog search & embedded book previews.
    • PoetryDB (poetrydb.org) — Classic English poetry anthologies & poet lookup.
    • MangaDex (api.mangadex.org) — Live manga catalog, English chapter feeds, and page streaming.
    • Jikan v4 (api.jikan.moe) — MyAnimeList manga rankings, scores, genres, and synopses.
    • Internet Archive (archive.org) — Golden & Silver Age comics, vintage books, CBZ/EPUB unpacking & BookReader.

    Optional Local Proxy Server

    Checking...

    Some external hosts enforce browser CORS restrictions on raw .cbz/.epub downloads or chapter image batches. When available, the local server (Books/WEB BOOKS/start-server.bat) proxies them directly; otherwise automatic CORS fallbacks are used.
