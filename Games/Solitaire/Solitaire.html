How to Play Klondike Solitaire

Master the timeless card rules in under a minute
🂡 🂱 🃁 🃑

1. Build the 4 Foundations

Move Aces up to the four top-right Foundation piles and build each suit upward in ascending order from Ace → 2 → 3 … → Queen → King.

♠︎K ♥︎Q ♣︎J ♦︎10

2. Stack Alternating Colors

In the seven Tableau columns, place cards in descending rank with alternating colors (Red on Black, Black on Red). Only a King (or King-led stack) can fill an empty column.

🂠

3. Draw & Recycle Stock

Click the top-left Stock pile to flip 1 or 3 cards into the Waste pile. Uncover face-down tableau cards whenever possible to open more lines of play.

♛︎

4. Scoring & Controls

+10 to Foundation, +5 Waste to Tableau or flipping a hidden card. Drag stacks smoothly, double-click to auto-move, or enable One-Tap Move in Options.
